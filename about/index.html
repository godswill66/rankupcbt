Powering Africa's Next Generation of Scholars

RankUp CBT was born from a simple belief: every JAMB candidate in Africa deserves equal access to real-time performance insights, smart practice tools, and the motivation that comes from seeing your national rank improve every day.

Mission & Vision

Mission

To democratize JAMB preparation by providing free, accurate, real-time national ranking and intelligent practice tools so every student — regardless of location or resources — can compete confidently and secure admission into their dream university.

Vision

To become the most trusted and widely used JAMB preparation platform in Nigeria and West Africa, where data-driven insights replace guesswork, and every serious student knows exactly where they stand — and how to get to the top.

Our Promise

100% free forever • Updated 2026 syllabus & past questions • Real-time national ranking • No hidden fees • Built for Nigerian students, by Nigerian students
